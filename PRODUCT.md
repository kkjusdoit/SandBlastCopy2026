# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Mobile players who want a fast, readable falling-block game that can be controlled comfortably with one or two thumbs.

## Product Purpose

Flow Sand combines falling-block placement with granular sand simulation. The interface should keep the board, score, next piece, and four core actions understandable at a glance without competing with play.

## Brand Personality

Focused, tactile, and quietly arcade-like. The game should feel energetic through responsive input and colorful pieces, not through decorative visual noise.

## Anti-references

Avoid crowded neon arcade dashboards, ornamental sci-fi chrome, overlapping HUD labels, tiny touch targets, and motion that obscures the board or delays input.

## Design Principles

- Keep the board visually dominant at every supported aspect ratio.
- Make score, best score, speed, and next piece readable without scanning.
- Give every gameplay action a generous, consistent touch target.
- Use color to communicate hierarchy and game state, not as decoration.
- Keep transitions brief and preserve immediate control feedback.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain strong text contrast, touch targets of at least 44 logical pixels, readable labels, and gameplay that does not depend on color alone. Respect reduced-motion expectations by avoiding decorative animation.
